Door stickers

One sticker per session. Write each address to an NFC tag (any NFC writer app does it) and stick it by the door with this card. Phones without NFC can scan the QR code. Neither needs an app.

Phones can't open 127.0.0.1 or localhost. Start Crew with CREW_HOST=0.0.0.0 and put your laptop's network address above, like http://192.168.1.20:8765.
